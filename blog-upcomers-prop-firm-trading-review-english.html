Understanding the Prop Firm Model (Funded Accounts)

Proprietary Trading (Prop Firms) allows retail traders to manage significant capital without risking their personal savings. After purchasing and passing an evaluation test called a "Challenge", the Prop Firm assesses your risk management skills. Upon successful completion, you are granted a funded account (often up to $100,000 or more) and keep the majority of the profits generated (up to 90% depending on the firm).
